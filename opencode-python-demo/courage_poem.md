# The Brave Little Spark

When shadows stretch and grow too tall,
And doubts begin to creep,
A tiny spark inside us all
Is waking from its sleep.

It doesn't roar, it doesn't shout,
It whispers, soft and low:
"You've done hard things before, no doubt,
So try — and watch it grow."

Courage isn't never being scared,
It's standing when you shake.
It's saying, "Yes, I'm unprepared,"
Then trying for your sake.

It's raising up your hand in class,
It's speaking kind and true,
It's helping someone when they fall,
It's being simply you.

So when the mountain looks too steep,
And fear says, "Turn away,"
Remember promises you keep,
And take one step — today.

For every hero starts out small,
With trembling hands and knees,
And bravest of them all
Is the one who still believes.
