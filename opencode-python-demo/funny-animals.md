# The Wacky Animal Ball

The pig wore boots and danced a jig,
The goat wore socks and danced with Zig.
The cow said, "Moo, I want to sing!"
The duck wore slippers on each wing.

The cat wore sunglasses at night,
The frog wore pants that were too tight.
The dog did somersaults for fun,
The hen laid eggs, then counted one!

The sheep said, "Baa, let's jump and hop!"
The horse did cartwheels till he'd drop.
The mouse wore a hat that was big,
The elephant danced with the pig.

So if you hear a moo or neigh,
Just clap your hands and shout, "Hooray!"
The animals all laugh and play,
And dance the wacky night away!
