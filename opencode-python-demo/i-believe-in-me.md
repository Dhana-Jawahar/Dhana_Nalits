# I Believe in Me

*An original, uplifting pop song for teenagers (13+).*

## Verse 1

I woke up to the mirror, didn't like what I saw,
Every flaw a little louder than the dreams I'd drawn.
But I'm done apologizing for the space I take,
I'm building something real from every last mistake.

## Pre-Chorus

'Cause the doubt in my head isn't who I am,
Every stumble's a spark, every fall is a plan.
And I'm learning to stand when it's easier to run,
This is only the start — I'm not nearly done.

## Chorus

I believe in me, even on the dark days,
I believe in me, when the world won't make way.
Let 'em talk, let 'em stare, I'm not backing down,
I believe in me, and I'm owning it now.

## Verse 2

Yeah, the halls get heavy and the whispers get sharp,
They don't know the strength it took to heal this heart.
But I'm not the kid who needed their okay,
I got my own light and I'm lighting my way.

## Bridge

And if you feel small tonight, just hold on tight,
The smallest flame still cuts through the darkest night.
You don't have to be perfect, just hold your ground,
One step at a time — look at you now.

## Final Chorus

I believe in me, even on the dark days,
I believe in me, when the world won't make way.
Let 'em talk, let 'em stare, I'm not backing down,
I believe in me — yeah, I believe in me now.
I believe in me, and I'm owning it now.
